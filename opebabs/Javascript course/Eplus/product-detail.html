Product Description

iPhone 13 Pro. Super Retina XDR display with ProMotion. Huge camera upgrade for dramatic new possibilities. Exceptional durability. The ultrafast A15 Bionic chip. And breakthrough battery life. Let's Pro.


iPhone 13 Pro takes a huge leap forward, bringing incredible speed to everything you do and delivering our best photos and videos ever.


Key Features:

  • 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR display with ProMotion for a faster, more responsive feel
  • Cinematic mode adds shallow depth of field and shifts focus automatically in your videos
  • Pro camera system with new 12MP Telephoto, Wide, and Ultra Wide cameras; LiDAR Scanner; 6x optical zoom range; macro photography; Photographic Styles, ProRes video, Smart HDR 4, Night mode, Apple ProRAW, 4K Dolby Vision HDR recording
  • A15 Bionic chip with new 5-core GPU for lightning-fast performance
  • Up to 28 hours of video playback, the best battery life ever in an iPhone
  • Durable design with Ceramic Shield
  • Industry-leading IP68 water resistance
  • 5G for superfast downloads and high-quality streaming
  • iOS 15 packs new features to do more with iPhone than ever before

Technical Specifications

Display 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR display with ProMotion technology, 2532 x 1170 resolution at 460 ppi
Processor A15 Bionic chip with 5-core GPU, 6-core CPU (2 performance and 4 efficiency cores), 16-core Neural Engine
Storage 256GB (also available in 128GB, 512GB, and 1TB)
Camera Pro 12MP camera system: Telephoto, Wide, and Ultra Wide cameras
Front Camera 12MP TrueDepth camera with Portrait mode, 4K Dolby Vision HDR recording
Battery Up to 28 hours video playback, up to 75 hours audio playback
Charging Fast-charge capable, MagSafe and Qi wireless charging
Water Resistance Rated IP68 (maximum depth of 6 meters up to 30 minutes)
Dimensions 146.7 mm x 71.5 mm x 7.65 mm
Weight 204 grams
Operating System iOS 15
